## Generation prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Create a production macOS Dock app icon based on the supplied Cheshi logo. Preserve exactly its distinctive cat-head silhouette, two ears, no eyes, and huge segmented curved grin. Center this white/silver softly raised, rounded neumorphic logo on a matte BLACK rounded-square app tile, straight-on orthographic view. Soft embossed relief, subtle silver edge light and soft contact shadows, polished restrained neumorphism. Logo occupies about 72% of tile width, balanced padding, strongly legible at small Dock sizes. Black tile with gently rounded macOS app-icon corners occupies about 88% of square image width. Outside tile fully transparent alpha. One single finished square icon, 1024x1024, no text, no mockup scene, no extra symbols, no Electron atom. Clean edges with no speckled artifacts. Keep the logo geometry faithful to the reference.
